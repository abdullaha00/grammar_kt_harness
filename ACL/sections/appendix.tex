\section{Generator and Measurement Detail}

The 18 generator KCs fall into five auditable groups:
\begin{itemize}
  \item finite-form selection: present and past;
  \item aspectual dependencies: perfect and progressive;
  \item voice/polarity: BE-passive and verbal negation;
  \item clause operations: imperative, polar question, and non-subject-WH; and
  \item central-modal identity: can, could, may, might, must, shall, should,
    will, and would.
\end{itemize}
Seventeen recur across cells.  Seven have fewer than six final items, but every
KC has at least two items and 12 seen acquisition opportunities per learner.
The perfect-progressive-chain alternative would be active in six cells but is
nested in both aspect dependencies; it was excluded before item generation.

The final bank contains 84 seen, 20 unseen-combination, and nine unseen-value
items.  Max-one preserves all 75 cells with 75 items, while max-three adds only
13 items beyond max-two.  No selected prompts are exact duplicates.  Surface
nonidentity is not itself evidence of pedagogical diversity.

\section{Research-Question Ledger}

\paragraph{RQ1---auditability supported; platform validity not established.}
Full-v1 is complete and replayable, but only 60/113 items are usable in both
automated audits, and the matched-format successor freezes only 5/38 families.
There is no new dataset release.

\paragraph{RQ2---supported for declared controls.}
Coarsening, refinement, and Q corruption harm prediction in clean full-v1.  In
the separate controlled scenario, planted format nuisance increases a false
split's relative advantage and an observed format term repairs the strong
control.  The correction is inconsistent under combined heterogeneity; the
aligned item basis is only a positive control.

\paragraph{RQ3---unique recovery rejected; information-design effects
supported.}
Equivalent seen-Q rules remain observationally indistinguishable, while
anchors restore selected contrasts.  Linked structured errors improve planted
failure localisation more than prediction; schedule changes alter model
recovery; open dialogue improves naturalness while weakening determinacy and
KC attribution.

The original grammar-generalisation result remains supporting evidence:
reusable KCs outperform exact-cell histories on pairwise-seen/full-tuple-unseen
cells, within a narrow synthetic regime.  Every answer is conditional on the
declared generator, instrument, assignment policy, and response-information
world.
