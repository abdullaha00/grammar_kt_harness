\section{Limitations and Ethics}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{No human measurement validation.}
Both full-bank audits, the KC inductions, the matched-bank critics, and the
dialogue pilot are model-based stress tests.  No learner, teacher,
psychometrician, or platform reviewer assessed the rendered tasks.  Role
disagreement is informative but is not inter-rater reliability, and plurality
is not validity.  CEFR appropriateness, accessibility, bias, scorer coverage,
and actual response processes remain unmeasured.  Accordingly there is
\textbf{no new dataset release}; full-v1 remains the clean reference and none of
the extension artifacts is classroom-ready or release-valid.

\paragraph{Item surface and matched-bank failure.}
Full-v1 learners never read prompts or produce text: item IDs select Q rows and
binary outcomes are sampled from mastery.  The 113-item audit therefore cannot
change its responses.  The intended 152-slot matched bank failed its
preregistered complete-family, coverage, and rank gates.  The 20 passing slots
are not a representative partial bank.  A future declared-correction layer
would need independent replay and then rendered human/expert and learner
validation before any deployment or release claim.

\paragraph{Content-free controlled scenario.}
The quantitative format/item study uses structural slots and categorical
format labels, not learner-facing content.  Planted effects are sensitivity
levels rather than estimates from human data.  Orthogonality holds over equally
weighted seen slots, not exactly over the acquisition event multiset.  Model D
is aligned to the planted same-seen-item residual basis; held-out item effects
are encoded as zero.  Its advantage does not demonstrate arbitrary item
deconfounding or unseen-item generalisation.  Explicit format adjustment is not
consistently superior to the false split after learner heterogeneity is added.

\paragraph{Errors and learner state.}
The structured failed-KC label is sampled after an incorrect outcome in
proportion to active mastery deficit.  It is neither causal truth nor a
validated learner-error taxonomy; the 80\% condition hides labels rather than
misclassifying them.  The Beta evidence-count terminal-KC estimate is not a
fitted mastery trajectory, and its improvement under shuffled labels exposes
metric bias.  We therefore do not scale to synthetic error text or claim that
error-aware KT recovers human knowledge.

\paragraph{Platform schedules.}
Q-balanced, curriculum, and mixed policies share one occurrence multiset and,
under order-independent learning, identical terminal oracle mastery.  Their
fitted differences concern history representation, not learning efficacy.
Adaptive assignment changes exposure and state; its logged propensity is tied
to the deterministic policy and is not a general off-policy-evaluation weight.
The alternative-policy fits were frozen after responses and are exploratory.

\paragraph{Statistical uncertainty.}
Learner bootstrap intervals condition on one fitted model, frozen train/dev
sample, bank, seed, hyperparameter, and simulator world.  They omit refitting,
item/cell sampling, bank construction, simulator-family and seed-level
uncertainty, multiplicity, and any human population.  Three-seed ranges are
descriptive.  Fitted terminal learner--KC recovery was not implemented for the
new A--D models; item-prerequisite state and evidence-count diagnostics must not
be relabelled as that estimand.

\paragraph{Linguistic and generalisation scope.}
The empirical domain is English single-main-clause verbal morphosyntax under
six dimensions.  Source normalisation is model-assisted and has no expert
adjudication or second-language replication.  The six unseen-value cells all
concern perfect-progressive aspect, and every generator KC has seen support.
Neither the alternate-schema software test nor this narrow probe establishes
cross-lingual or unrestricted grammatical generalisation.

\paragraph{Ethics.}
No human-participant or learner records are used; all response histories are
synthetic.  Latent conditions are modelling assumptions, not demographic or
ability groups.  Generated exercises and error forms must not be used for
assessment or instruction without qualified review for correctness,
accessibility, fairness, and educational value.  The consult-only source is not
redistributed, and its use remains subject to its licence.
