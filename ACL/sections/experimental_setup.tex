\section{Experimental Design}
\label{sec:experimental-design}

The base run uses the factorized policy.  Two variants reuse its frozen source,
normalization, canonical, and realization artifacts, changing only the KC
policy and downstream stages.  Because construction makes five items per KC,
the resulting item pools are policy-specific; Q-matrix structure is directly
comparable, whereas predictive scores are not a controlled estimate of a
policy effect.

The simulator is a transparent integration test.  It draws independent initial
mastery for 60 learners from each of three profiles with
$\mathrm{Beta}(2,6)$, $\mathrm{Beta}(2,2)$, and $\mathrm{Beta}(6,2)$ priors.
Every learner receives two shuffled passes over the relevant accepted item
pool.  For active KCs $A_i$, mastery $m_{lk}$, deterministic item difficulty
$d_i\in[-0.8,0.8]$, and multi-KC penalty $0.15$, the response logit is
\begin{equation}
 z_{li}=|A_i|^{-1}\!\sum_{k\in A_i}\operatorname{logit}(m_{lk})
       -d_i-0.15(|A_i|-1),
\end{equation}
and $p_{li}=0.1+0.8\sigma(z_{li})$.  Mastery increases after every response at
a profile-specific rate.  Each learner is split chronologically 60/20/20.
Latent mastery, probabilities, random draws, and profile labels are written to
separate oracle files and rejected from KT input.

We evaluate a Beta-smoothed empirical KC rate, fixed-parameter BKT
($P(T)=.04$, $P(G)=.20$, $P(S)=.10$), and $L_2$-regularized logistic regression.
The latter uses only pre-event learner success, active-KC success, prior
opportunities, item difficulty, KC count, and one-hot KC indicators.  We run
seeds 20260817--20260821 and report mean test AUC and log loss with 95\% $t$
intervals over the five runs.  Accuracy and validation metrics are retained in
the artifact but are secondary here.
