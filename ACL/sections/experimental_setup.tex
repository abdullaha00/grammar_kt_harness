\section{Experimental Design}
\label{sec:experimental-design}

\subsection{Clean-control representation evidence}

The immutable full-v1 stream supports the known-truth baseline.  A standardized
PFA-like logistic model uses strictly prior successes and opportunities on
active hypothesis KCs \citep{pavlik-etal-2009}.  It receives no item identity,
generator state, response probability, random draw, or probe outcome.  Six
deterministic representations span one all-merged KC, six family unions,
\kstar{}, 35 split-2 KCs, 66 split-4 KCs, and 75 exact cells.  Nine frozen
Q-corruption controls add false-positive, false-negative, or mixed edges at a
27-edge budget.  All representations reuse the same 113,000 probes.

Observable-only discovery searches 181 outcome-independent features,
operations, unions, splits, supported intersections, exact cells, and hash
distractors using learner-disjoint fit/validation groups.  It cannot read
\kstar{}, \qstar{}, oracle state, or probes before selection.  Name-free
activation matching opens truth only after the representation is frozen.
Separate collection controls vary learner count, opportunities, within-cell
replication, and A-only/B-only/A+B anchors.  These retained experiments answer
whether response volume or Q geometry distinguishes hypotheses; they do not
validate the learner-facing instrument.

\subsection{Audit and construction outcomes}

The two item audits cover all 113 full-v1 prompts.  The strict census is one
rubric-guided review; the second comprises four role-specific fixed calls per
item.  A deterministic script maps their labels, reports exact disagreement,
and preserves item IDs for every cell.  The KC audit is outcome-free.  Three KC
inductions use independent calls and are compared by activation signatures.

The matched-bank campaign treats a complete cell/variant family as the unit:
all four formats must pass deterministic checks, independent solver coverage,
and three role critics.  Three declared rounds may generate new candidates but
may not edit prior output.  The primary outcome is the number of complete
families and resulting cell/KC support and Q rank; isolated passing slots cannot
satisfy the release gate.

\subsection{A--D nuisance comparison}

The controlled instrument contains 144 seen and eight held-out structural
slots.  For each of six worlds and three seeds, 500 learners generate 170,000
events under a Q-balanced schedule.  Nine additional response runs use the
combined heterogeneous world with curriculum, mixed, or adaptive assignment,
for 4.59 million events overall.

Learners are split 60/20/20 by a stable hash.  Model selection uses training
learners' acquisition rows and dev learners' seen probes; the selected penalty
is refit on train+dev acquisition and evaluated on test learners' terminal
probes.  Standardization is fit on training acquisition only.  Primary scope is
seen-probe log loss; Brier score, fixed-width ECE, AUC, and an oracle-only
item-prerequisite state RMSE are secondary.  Model D's item basis covers seen
item IDs only; held-out probe items are zero encoded.

The four preregistered contrasts are:
\begin{align*}
&[(L_B-L_A)_{\text{strong format}}-(L_B-L_A)_{\text{zero}}],\\
&(L_C-L_B)_{\text{strong format}},\\
&(L_D-L_C)_{\text{item only}},\qquad
(L_D-L_C)_{\text{item+format}} .
\end{align*}
Negative values in the first contrast mean format nuisance increases B's
relative advantage over A; they do not validate the false split.  The remaining
negative values favour the explicitly adjusted candidate.

\subsection{Error, policy, and dialogue evidence}

Error-history models hold D fixed and vary only prior observable error labels.
We evaluate prediction, item-prerequisite state, failed-KC localisation on
incorrect multi-KC probes, and a transparent Beta-smoothed terminal-KC evidence
diagnostic.  The latter is not a fitted KT state.  The shuffled stream preserves
within-item/phase category marginals and is a negative learner-specific
information control, not a claim of complete statistical independence.

Schedule diagnostics report acquisition and probe accuracy separately, item/KC
exposure inequality, repetition gap, and adjacency.  Q-balanced, curriculum,
and mixed schedules share the same occurrence multiset; under unconditional
order-independent learning they have identical terminal oracle mastery by
construction.  Alternative-policy A--D fits were frozen after responses and
descriptive diagnostics, so they are exploratory.

The dialogue study generates four matched families at five openness levels and
collects five role judgments for each of 20 opportunities.  We report each
dimension and disagreement; no composite is computed.

\subsection{Uncertainty and model assistance}

Within a frozen fitted model, percentile intervals resample 82 test learners
2,000 times.  They condition on the fit, selected hyperparameter, bank, seed,
and simulator world; they omit refitting, item/cell sampling, simulator-family
uncertainty, and multiplicity.  Three-seed means and ranges are descriptive,
not population intervals.

Retained construction and audits use fixed \texttt{gpt-5.6-sol} or
\texttt{gpt-5.6-terra} calls with prompt, input, schema, settings, raw output,
parsed output, and hashes frozen.  Model critics are independent stress tests,
not human participants.  No learner, teacher, or platform expert judgment is
fabricated.
