\section{Discussion}

\paragraph{Known truth is necessary, not sufficient.}
Full-v1 supplies the counterfactual quantities that ordinary learner logs do
not: exact opportunity boundaries, generator KCs, Q, mastery trajectories, and
paired response worlds.  That makes representation errors measurable.  It does
not make a stored prompt understandable, its accepted-answer set fair, or its
response process human-like.  The audits and failed matched-bank construction
are therefore part of the scientific result, not ancillary quality control.
They show that artifact integrity, Q geometry, and measurement validity answer
different questions.

\paragraph{RQ1: construction requires explicit validity layers.}
The grammar-to-cell stage remains useful because it preserves scope and
uncertainty without turning every descriptor into a KC.  The next two stages
are less settled.  Independent KC proposals agree on only nine activation
signatures, while item critics disagree by role on half the bank.  A nominally
simple item may instantiate its cell yet remain underdetermined, demand an
undeclared operation, or encode an annotation protocol instead of a learner
interface.  Conversely, a natural-looking task can provide weak evidence about
the target.  A credible construction pipeline must therefore gate linguistic
fidelity, answerability, KC purity, and deployability separately; no average
``realism'' score or full-rank Q can compensate for a critical failure.

\paragraph{RQ2: nuisance can masquerade as skill structure.}
In the clean control, unnecessary KC splits lose to shared \kstar{}.  After a
format effect is planted, the format-specific split improves because it can
absorb measurement variance, even though learner mastery remains shared.  An
observable format term performs better in that strong control.  This is direct
counterfactual evidence for a familiar interpretive risk: predictive support
for a finer KC ontology need not imply distinct learnable competencies.
However, the correction is scenario-dependent.  It is not consistent in the
combined heterogeneous world, and the item-only experiment does not reproduce
the legacy split reversal.  Model D succeeds because the planted effect lies
in its same-seen-item basis by construction.  It is an oracle-aligned positive
control, not a solution for new items or arbitrary real-platform difficulty.

\paragraph{RQ3: information design sets the inferential ceiling.}
Activation-equivalent ontologies cannot be separated by more responses on the
same Q rows.  Anchor contrasts can restore structural distinguishability, but
weak effects can remain practically hard to detect.  Structured error labels
can localise a planted failed KC while barely changing response prediction;
the shuffled control further shows that a convenient state diagnostic can
improve for the wrong reason.  Assignment policies select different histories,
so recovery depends on the platform process as well as the learner.  Finally,
the dialogue continuum exposes the same tension at the item surface: open
interaction looks more conversational while multiplying reasonable responses,
incidental grammar, shortcuts, and attribution uncertainty.  Ecological
plausibility and measurement precision are axes to report, not endpoints of a
single scale.

\paragraph{Implications for real data collection.}
The results motivate crossed KCs and formats, multiple semantic variants,
diagnostically distinct anchors, stable item identifiers, explicit response
mechanisms and accepted-answer policies, raw learner responses, error and
context metadata, and the assignment policy or propensity that produced each
opportunity.  Item and format effects should be modelled before interpreting a
KC split as learner structure.  Non-updating probes can help separate
measurement from practice where ethically and operationally feasible.  These
are design principles and failure-mode checks; synthetic effect sizes and
sample thresholds are not literal platform prescriptions.

\paragraph{What should be validated next.}
The declared human/expert protocol separates six targets: GrammarCell fidelity,
KC pedagogical plausibility, item deployability, learner answerability, format
plausibility, and error realism.  It uses rendered tasks, role-separated expert
review, and a balanced learner solvability study.  Such evidence is necessary
before deploying a successor bank.  The failed automated campaign is useful
because it identifies where declared correction and revalidation are needed;
it is not a rationale to lower the release gate.

\paragraph{Language-independence boundary.}
Projection, Q auditing, simulation, perturbation, and evaluation are
schema-driven, and a non-English-named toy schema exercises their interfaces.
EGP parsing, the six dimensions, English operation declarations, prompts, and
error interpretations remain language-specific.  This is reusable methodology,
not cross-lingual empirical validity or a universal grammar ontology.
