\section{Grammar-to-KT Pipeline}
\label{sec:method}

\subsection{Normalization and canonicalization}

Each annotation unit is processed in a fresh model context containing the
closed schema, rulebook, phase instructions, and JSON schema.  A validator
checks fields, domains, status invariants, and tense--modal and imperative
compatibility.  Every attempt retains its prompt, raw and parsed output,
invocation metadata, and errors.

Only a partial Phase~1 result can normally enter Phase~2.  Its note names the
non-scalar dimensions eligible for refinement; Phase~2 additionally sees the
source examples.  The transition validator freezes exact and ineligible
dimensions, requires every new cell to descend from a Phase~1 cell, and forbids
unlicensed list expansion.  Contradictory evidence produces an unresolved
result rather than silently changing an exact value.

Complete mappings are serialized in a fixed dimension order and assigned a
content-derived identifier.  Equal tuples therefore deduplicate independently
of source identity or order.  A canonical inventory stores each tuple once;
an edge table separately records every source mapping cell, its descriptor and
position.  Deduplication consequently preserves many-to-one provenance.

\subsection{Realization and KC policies}

The realizer pairs each canonical cell with a validated predicate frame,
subject features, and any WH or imperative conditions.  A ten-frame lexicon
supplies inflectional forms and argument structure.  An ordered procedure
constructs modal, perfect, progressive, passive, and lexical chains before
applying negation, DO-support, inversion, WH-fronting, or imperative rules.
The retained derivation includes tokens, agreement site, auxiliary chain, and
named operations.

A KC opportunity joins a canonical cell, one realization, its operations,
split, and source provenance.  We compare three declarative policies.  The
\emph{factorized} policy names marked reusable decisions such as finite tense,
aspect, passive voice, negation, questions, imperatives, and individual modals.
The \emph{interaction} policy adds five named combinations: DO-support in
questions and negation, perfect--progressive, progressive--passive, and
perfect--passive chains.  The \emph{whole-cell} policy assigns one KC to each
exact six-dimensional cell.  It is a high-resolution structural baseline, not
a cognitive claim.  Every policy materializes a frozen cell--KC projection;
leaving a cell without a KC is fatal.

\subsection{Items and Q-matrices}

For every activated KC, the constructor searches eligible cells, source
conditions, predicate frames, and subjects in stable order to create five
unique controlled-transformation items.  A prompt exposes the intended grammar
and fixed lexical material; the accepted answer is the deterministic
realization.  The first two replicates are development items and the remaining
three are held out.

Deterministic checks cover record shape, provenance, realization, punctuation,
uniqueness, and agreement with the frozen projection.  Passing candidates
receive an independent automated diagnostic for plausibility, naturalness,
unsupported constructions, world-knowledge dependence, and answer ambiguity.
This diagnostic is not human validation.  For accepted item $i$ and KC $k$,
\begin{equation}
  Q_{ik}=\mathbb{I}[k\in\mathcal{K}(c_i)],
\end{equation}
where $\mathcal{K}(c_i)$ is the frozen projection of the item's cell.  Unknown
identifiers, empty rows, and label--projection disagreements are fatal;
density, row width, repeated columns, and low support remain reportable
diagnostics rather than triggers for hidden repair.

For example, the source descriptor for affirmative lexical yes/no questions
maps to a cell with present tense, no aspect, active voice, positive polarity,
a polar-question clause, and no modal.  The canonicalizer assigns that tuple a
content-derived identifier; one generated item is ``Does the technician review
the proposal?''; and the factorized projection activates finite-present and
polar-question KCs.  The edge record retains the original descriptor
throughout this chain.
