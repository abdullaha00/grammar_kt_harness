\section{Reproducibility and Audit Trail}

The immutable reference is rooted at
\nolinkurl{data/grammar_kt_full_v1/}.  Its manifest records the pre-response
revision, exact simulator command, configurations, inputs, deterministic gzip
metadata, row counts, and an 88-file recursive inventory.  Independent replay
reconstructs the $113\times18$ Q and all 283,000 linked public/private events.
The measurement programme stores a separate baseline anchor containing the
protected tree and core hashes; no extension command writes inside full-v1.

Every LLM-backed scientific stage freezes its prompt, exact input, provider
schema, model and reasoning setting, raw response, parsed response, and hashes.
The complete item audit, three KC inductions, matched-bank attempts, and
dialogue critics retain role-specific output rather than a collapsed score.
Infrastructure failures are labelled separately from scientific failures.  In
particular, the matched-bank negative result is replayed from its immutable
candidate, solver, critic, and curation evidence.

Deterministic scripts under \nolinkurl{scripts/experiments/} reconstruct the
cross-audit synthesis, bank funnel, controlled worlds, structured-error
comparisons, and exploratory policy recovery.  The controlled-scenario study
plan hashes the structural instrument and implementation before responses.
Observable archives exclude mastery, Q, response probabilities, and planted
failure attributions; oracle archives are opened only by declared evaluations.
Cross-world contrasts verify learner sets and exact evaluation-row identity.

Because the controlled response archives contain 4.59 million events, the
programme-level evidence package retains compact aggregates, manifests, exact
hash contracts, and reconstruction commands rather than treating generated
rows as a new released dataset.  The final allow-list manifest also excludes
failed-attempt scratch trees, caches, and incomplete staging artifacts.  The
executable measurement notebook reads only retained compact evidence, and the
paper build, notebook execution, full test suite, and full-v1 replays are part
of final verification.
