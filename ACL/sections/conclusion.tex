\section{Conclusion}

\system turns prose grammar descriptors into inspectable KT artifacts while
preserving ambiguity and provenance.  Fresh runs show that normalization is the
main coverage bottleneck and that plausible KC policies yield substantially
different Q-matrices.  All three support reproducible synthetic KT checks, but
those checks cannot select a cognitively valid representation.  The next step
is expert validation followed by evaluation on a shared item pool and human
learner sequences.
