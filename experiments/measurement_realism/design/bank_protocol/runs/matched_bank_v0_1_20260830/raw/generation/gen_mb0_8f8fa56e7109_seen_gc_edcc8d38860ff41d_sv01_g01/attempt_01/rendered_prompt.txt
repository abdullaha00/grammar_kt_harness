FAMILY TASK

create exactly four learner-facing exercises that elicit or recognize that same

1. constrained_cloze

4. sentence_transformation

All four exercises must retain:

- the same GrammarCell;
- the same intended grammatical operations;

  keyed response.

The formats may change what is visible, but may not redefine the construct.

LEARNER-FACING PRINCIPLES

- Write concise instructions that a learner at `intended_cefr` can understand
  without linguistic terminology.
- Use common, concrete, transparent vocabulary. Do not make vocabulary, cultural
  knowledge, specialist knowledge, or semantic puzzles determine success.
- Give enough context to make the intended meaning and response clear.
- Do not display the completed target or the exact missing target-bearing span
  outside the response space.
- Make every reasonable equivalent response scorable. Enumerate ordinary
  contraction and punctuation variants when they preserve the target.
- A learner should not need to guess what the author intended.
- Do not use an artificial annotation instruction such as “produce a clause
  with feature X.” Phrase the task as a plausible lesson, practice, or review
  interaction.
- Do not include feedback text, learner outcomes, difficulty estimates, or
  claims about human cognition.

FORMAT CONTRACTS

`constrained_cloze`

- `response_mode` is `short_text`.
- `format_payload.sentence_template` contains exactly one literal token
  `[[RESPONSE]]`.
- The response slot contains all overt material needed to exercise the target
  operations. Do not reduce the task to copying visible morphology.
- Inserting `scoring.target_response` must yield exactly the canonical target
  sentence (apart from normalized outer spacing).

`dialogue_completion`

- `response_mode` is `short_text`.
- Supply at most three complete preceding turns and one incomplete turn.
- `format_payload.incomplete_turn_template` contains exactly one literal token
  `[[RESPONSE]]`.
- The dialogue must make the missing response pragmatically natural and
  sufficiently determinate.
- Inserting the keyed response into the incomplete turn must yield the exact
  canonical target sentence as the utterance text.

`multiple_choice`

- `response_mode` is `single_choice`.
- Supply exactly four options with IDs `A`, `B`, `C`, `D`.
- Exactly one option text equals the canonical target sentence and its option ID
  is `scoring.target_response` and `scoring.correct_choice_id`.
- Distractors preserve lexical meaning and differ primarily through plausible
  failures of the declared target operations. Do not use joke options, length
  clues, vocabulary traps, or several defensible answers.
- `distractor_annotations` are oracle-only and must name the intended operation
  error for each of the three non-keyed options.

`sentence_transformation`

- `response_mode` is `full_sentence`.
- Give a source sentence or pair of simple statements, a plain communicative
  instruction, and all needed lexical cues.
- The learner must not invent a synonym or new content word.
- `scoring.target_response` must equal the canonical target sentence.

ANSWER REPRESENTATION

For every item:

- `scoring.accepted_responses` contains response-widget strings, not prose
  explanations.
- The first accepted response is exactly `scoring.target_response`.
- `scoring.completed_target` is exactly `canonical_target_sentence`.
- Do not put full-sentence alternatives into a span-only cloze/dialogue answer
  set unless the widget asks for the full sentence.
- The declared normalizer handles only Unicode NFC, surrounding/internal
  whitespace, case, and terminal punctuation. List contractions or other
  non-trivial alternatives explicitly.
