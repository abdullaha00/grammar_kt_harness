# English controlled-production generation rules

- Make subject–verb agreement overt where the selected subject permits it.
- Realise simple present/past on the finite main verb unless an auxiliary is
  required; use DO-support for lexical-verb negation and interrogation.
- Order the auxiliary chain as modal, perfect HAVE, progressive BE, passive BE,
  lexical verb. Use the corresponding base, participle, or -ing forms.
- Realise canonical passive with BE plus a past participle and a compatible
  transitive predicate.
- Place NOT after the finite operator. Insert DO only when no other operator is
  present.
- In polar and non-subject-WH questions, invert the finite operator and subject.
  Subject-WH questions do not invert solely because the subject is WH.
- Central modals take a base-form complement and never co-occur with
  morphological present/past in the GrammarCell.
- Imperatives use a base verb. Ordinary negative imperatives use DO-support;
  LET'S NOT has `negation`, `imperative`, and `let_imperative` operation tags
  but not `do_support`.
- Use only these operation tags when relevant: `perfect`, `progressive`,
  `be_passive`, `central_modal`, `do_support`, `negation`,
  `operator_inversion`, `subject_wh`, `wh_fronting`, `imperative`,
  `emphatic_do`, `let_imperative`.
- Prefer common concrete vocabulary, avoid specialist knowledge, and keep all
  accepted answers genuinely equivalent for the target.
