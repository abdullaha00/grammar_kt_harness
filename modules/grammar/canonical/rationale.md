# Why this GrammarCell

The English pilot treats a grammar target as the conjunction of six directly
interpretable dimensions: tense, aspect, voice, polarity, clause type, and
central modal identity. The representation is a research hypothesis, not a
claim that these dimensions are cognitively atomic.

The cell is deliberately narrower than the EGP evidence. Source categories,
guidewords, can-do statements, examples, and CEFR labels are used to justify
normalisation but disappear from downstream records. Exact duplicate cells are
collapsed while their source identifiers are retained.

The representation excludes lexical aspect, agreement, transitivity,
auxiliary-operation labels, dialogue form, and item wording. Those may affect
English generation or candidate KC hypotheses, but they are not additional
canonical grammar dimensions.
