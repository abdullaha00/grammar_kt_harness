# Generator-KC rationale

The baseline generator ontology is a declared synthetic truth, not a claim
about human cognitive atoms. It is fixed from the canonical grammar inventory
before learner outcomes exist.

The declaration uses reusable English grammatical operations. Present and
past remain distinct finite-form choices. Perfect and progressive are shared
dependencies, so a perfect-progressive cell activates both instead of gaining
an atomic aspect-value KC. Passive, negative, imperative, and question values
are named by the grammatical operation they require. Each observed central
modal receives a distinct selection KC. Active, positive, declarative, simple,
modal-free, and tenseless values are reference conditions rather than extra
latent skills.

This hybrid is preferred for structural investigation because it preserves
reuse and compositional contrasts without appending linearly dependent
superordinate operations to a complete feature-value encoding. Pairwise and
exact-cell KCs remain downstream hypotheses. No response-selected KC can enter
this declaration.

The perfect-progressive-chain interaction is the only preregistered optional
addition. It has an independent linguistic interpretation, but it will remain
outside the baseline unless the full bank supplies multiple contexts and a
small independent-mastery positive control can recover it separately from its
perfect and progressive parents.
