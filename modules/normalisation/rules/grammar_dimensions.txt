EGP normalization schema version: v1-pilot-frozen

GrammarCell:
  tense:
    present | past | NA
    | list[present | past | NA]
    | null

  aspect:
    none | progressive | perfect | perfect_progressive
    | list[none | progressive | perfect | perfect_progressive]
    | null

  voice:
    active | passive
    | list[active | passive]
    | null

  polarity:
    positive | negative
    | list[positive | negative]
    | null

  clause:
    declarative
    | polar_question
    | subject_wh_question
    | non_subject_wh_question
    | imperative
    | list[
        declarative,
        polar_question,
        subject_wh_question,
        non_subject_wh_question,
        imperative
      ]
    | null

  modal:
    none
    | can | could | may | might | must
    | shall | should | will | would
    | list[
        none,
        can, could, may, might, must,
        shall, should, will, would
      ]
    | null


EGPMapping:
  egp_id: string

  result:
    complete
    | partial
    | out_of_scope
    | schema_failure
    | unresolved

  cells: list[GrammarCell]

  note: string | null


SEMANTICS

- scalar = exact value established by the source
- list = source constrains the value to this set; members are NOT independently
  asserted as supported
- null = source does not establish this dimension
- dimensions inside a cell are AND constraints
- cells in `cells` are OR branches
- never infer Cartesian combinations

Result invariants:

complete:
  - cells is non-empty
  - every value in every cell is scalar

partial:
  - cells is non-empty
  - at least one value is a list or null

out_of_scope | schema_failure | unresolved:
  - cells = []
  - note briefly explains why

Important:
  modal:none != modal:null
  tense:NA != tense:null
