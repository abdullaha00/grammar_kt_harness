and structurally distinguishable generator KCs without using learner outcomes?

## Fixed criteria

- linguistic coherence and declared activation semantics;
- reuse across cells;
- item and cell support;
- unique Q columns and full column rank;
- A-only/B-only/A+B contrasts;
- distinct cell activation patterns;
- parsimony;
- no learner outcomes or downstream predictive selection.

The optional chain must have at least three cells, preferably six independently
validated items, multiple surrounding contexts, full Q rank, and stable
independent-mastery recovery in a positive-control pilot. Otherwise the simpler
hybrid is frozen without interactions.
